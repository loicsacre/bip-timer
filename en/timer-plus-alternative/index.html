In October 2026, Timer Plus – Workouts Timer started asking for payment before a timer will start: $2.99 a month, or $19.99 for lifetime access, according to its App Store page. If all you need is an interval timer for your class or your own workouts, you do not have to pay.

THE SAME WORKOUT, SET UP IN TEN SECONDS

Exercises: how many exercises make one round, for example 4.

Work: how long each exercise lasts. With “Per exercise”, each one can have its own.

Rest exercises: the break between two exercises. Rest rounds: the longer break after each round.

Rounds: how many times the block repeats. Then Start: a beep at every change, a 3-2-1 countdown, and the screen changes colour with each phase.

The same: work and rest times, rounds, beeps and countdown, a screen that stays on, and it works offline once opened.

WHAT IT IS

An interval timer that describes a whole block: several exercises, several rounds, and separate rests between exercises and between rounds. It beeps, keeps the screen on and works offline.

STRUCTURE

Circuit: exercise 1, 2, 3, 4, then back to 1 for the next round.

By sets: every set of exercise 1, then every set of exercise 2, and so on.

UNIT

Time: work lasts a fixed time, the clock moves to the next step by itself.

Reps: the screen shows the number of repetitions and waits for you to press Done.

PER EXERCISE

Under Work (or Repetitions), “Per exercise” gives each exercise its own value. The + and − of the main row then shift every value by the same step.

RESTS

Between exercises, and between rounds (Circuit) or between sets (By sets). A value of 0 removes the step.

DURING THE SESSION

Get ready
Work
Rest

A beep on each of the last 3 seconds, a higher beep at every phase change.

SAMPLE SESSIONS

HIIT circuit: 4 exercises × 3 rounds, 40 s work, 20 s between exercises, 60 s between rounds. About 13 min.

Tabata: 1 exercise, 8 rounds, 20 s work, 10 s between rounds, 10 s to get ready. Exactly 4 min.

30/30 intervals: 1 exercise, 10 rounds, 30 s work, 30 s between rounds. About 10 min.

Strength in sets: By sets, unit Reps, 3 exercises × 4 sets of 12 reps, 90 s between sets, 2 min between exercises. The screen waits for you to confirm each set.

Mixed circuit: with “Per exercise”, 60 s of plank, 30 s of push-ups and 45 s of squats in the same block.

ON THE HOME SCREEN

iPhone: Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Android: browser menu, then “Install app”. It then opens without a connection.

PRIVACY

No account, no cookie. Your settings stay on your phone. An anonymous audience measure (GoatCounter) counts visits and sessions started, without ever identifying anyone.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is BIP Timer free?

Yes, completely: no ads, no account and no subscription.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It runs in the browser, on a phone or a computer. You can add it to your home screen: it then opens like an app, even without a connection.

Does the screen stay on during a session?

Yes, on recent browsers: Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android and on computers. If yours cannot, a message says so before you start.

Does it beep over my music?

Yes, the beeps mix with your music without stopping it. On iPhone, silent mode mutes the beeps unless you choose “Ring” under Silent mode: the music then stops during the session. On Android the phone also vibrates at each phase.

What if I lock my phone?

The browser pauses the page: no beeps while the screen is locked. The clock keeps counting and lands on the right step as soon as you come back.

How is it different from a regular HIIT timer?

A regular timer alternates work and rest. BIP Timer describes a whole block: several exercises, different rests between exercises and between rounds, a by-sets mode, reps to confirm and a work time of its own for each exercise.

Are my settings kept?

Yes, on your phone, until your next session. Nothing is sent anywhere.
